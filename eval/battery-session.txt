# Multi-turn inheritance invariants: what a follow-up keeps, what it must
# not drag along, and how game state feeds resolution. Run with:
#   gradlew evalRun -PevalSnapshot=eval\snapshot-sample.json -PevalBattery=eval\battery-session.txt -PevalRouteOnly=true
# Assertions assume the synthetic eval/snapshot-sample.json fixture (its
# slayer task is Bloodveld).

# "My task" resolves the task creature from the game capture, not from text.
what was my slayer task
ASSERT {"monsters": ["Bloodveld"], "skills": ["Slayer"]}

# A leading subject followed by a comma-spliced question still routes both
# the monster and the gear intent.
tormented demons, what gear should i use
ASSERT {"monsters": ["Tormented Demon"], "needs": ["strategy"]}

# "X or Y" comparisons resolve each side separately. The wiki really does
# redirect the span "blowpipe or..." to an ornament kit; that redirect must
# never win. "crystal armor" resolves to its own page, not the generic
# Armour page via desperation.
whats better blowpipe or bowfa with crystal armor
ASSERT {"items": ["Bow of Faerdhinen"], "pages": ["Crystal equipment"], "not_items": ["Trailblazer reloaded blowpipe ornament kit"], "not_pages": ["Armour"]}

# Conjunctions and articles must not be absorbed into entity spans.
should i use a whip or a scimitar
ASSERT {"pages": ["Whip", "Scimitar"]}

# A follow-up that only varies a parameter ("what about addy darts") keeps
# the monster subject from the previous turn, and the varied parameter
# resolves through the wiki's slang redirect (Addy darts -> Adamant dart).
# Vocab junk must not claim it first: the item index once carried the
# unobtainable "Dart", whose local hit on "darts" blocked the redirect.
tormented demons, blowpipe or bowfa without ca
ASSERT {"monsters": ["Tormented Demon"]}
> what about addy darts
ASSERT {"monsters": ["Tormented Demon"], "items": ["Adamant dart"], "not_items": ["Dart (unobtainable item)"]}

# A follow-up that resolves its own subject is self-contained: nothing from
# the previous turn leaks into its route, sourcing intent classifies, and
# the facility the answer hinges on is grounded from facts.
whats better blowpipe or bowfa with crystal armor
ASSERT {"items": ["Bow of Faerdhinen"]}
> whats the quickest way i can get planks?
ASSERT {"items": ["Plank"], "needs": ["item_sources"], "not_items": ["Bow of Faerdhinen"], "not_pages": ["Bow of Faerdhinen", "Crystal equipment"]}

# Anaphoric follow-ups ("what ONES") keep the previous subject even when
# they resolve a new entity of their own -- inheritance keys on
# pointing-back language, not on which entity kinds resolved. With context
# inherited, the desperation pass stays off: "bank" arrives via the
# facility rule (its Locations section), not as a bare page.
what is the difference between houses?
ASSERT {"pages": ["Player-owned house"]}
> what ones closer to a bank?
ASSERT {"pages": ["Player-owned house"], "facilities": ["Bank"], "not_pages": ["Bank"]}

# Locative "there" points back at the previous subject, so the follow-up
# keeps the monster even though it resolves its own item.
whats the best gear for vorkath
ASSERT {"monsters": ["Vorkath"]}
> is a tbow good there?
ASSERT {"monsters": ["Vorkath"], "items": ["Twisted bow"]}

# Existential "there" ("is there a...") points at nothing: a follow-up
# with its own subject starts clean, nothing inherits.
whats the best gear for vorkath
ASSERT {"monsters": ["Vorkath"]}
> is there a quick way to get to zulrah?
ASSERT {"monsters": ["Zulrah"], "not_monsters": ["Vorkath"]}

# Filler nouns in a follow-up must not become pages under desperation
# ("master list of all combined resources" once resolved pages Master,
# List, and Resources, crowding the sloop out of the page budget).
what materials do i need to build a sloop
ASSERT {"pages": ["Sloop"], "not_pages": ["Account"]}
> could you give me a master list of all combined resources that i require
ASSERT {"pages": ["Sloop"], "not_pages": ["Master", "List", "Resources"]}

# A resolved skill counts as this turn's subject: a self-contained skill
# question after an unrelated turn inherits nothing. (Skills once fell
# outside anyEntity(), so "how do I get started with sailing" inherited two
# items, three quests, and four pages -- 60k input tokens.)
Are there any quest items in my bank I don't need anymore?
ASSERT {"pages": ["Bank"]}
ANSWER A spare Ring of charos can go; both Creature of Fenkenstrain and Garden of Tranquillity are finished. The Crystal chime is only needed against warped creatures. It's recoverable from Dr Fenkenstrain. It also reduces charter and mine cart costs.
> Hm, how do I get started with sailing / what's the most efficient thing to start off with
ASSERT {"skills": ["Sailing"], "not_items": ["Ring of charos", "Crystal chime"], "not_quests": ["Creature of Fenkenstrain", "Garden of Tranquillity"], "not_pages": ["Dr Fenkenstrain", "Mine cart"]}

# An answer that opens with an enumeration (inventory list, route steps)
# must not hand the enumerated names to the next turn as inherited subject:
# only the prose preamble is subject. (A farming-run plan's inventory list
# once became three full item bundles and a quest bundle -- 85k input
# tokens on the follow-up.)
Can you orchestrate a farming run for me
ASSERT {"pages": ["Farming runs"]}
ANSWER Here's your farming run plan.\n- Magic secateurs (equip when harvesting)\n- Lunar staff for fairy rings\n- Ring of dueling(5) for banking\n1. Trollheim herb patch (Eadgar's Ruse done): plant Dwarf weed.
> how long does that take?
ASSERT {"pages": ["Farming runs"], "not_items": ["Magic secateurs", "Lunar staff", "Ring of dueling"], "not_quests": ["Eadgar's Ruse"]}

# A follow-up that restates the activity in its own words ("tree route")
# routes the guide page itself instead of inheriting the previous answer's
# supporting cast. "Ah" must stay an interjection (the wiki redirects it
# to Alchemical Hydra).
Can you orchestrate a farming run for me
ASSERT {"pages": ["Farming runs"]}
ANSWER Here's your farming run plan.\n- Magic secateurs (equip when harvesting)\n- Lunar staff for fairy rings\n- Ring of dueling(5) for banking\n1. Trollheim herb patch (Eadgar's Ruse done): plant Dwarf weed.
> Ah I meant a tree route specifically
ASSERT {"pages": ["Farming runs"], "not_items": ["Magic secateurs", "Lunar staff", "Ring of dueling"], "not_quests": ["Eadgar's Ruse"], "not_monsters": ["Alchemical Hydra"]}

# Two-letter slang ("cg") is this turn's own subject: nothing farming
# inherits into it. The correction after it points back with "i mean",
# keeping that subject while resolving its own entities ("bowfa", "ca").
can you give me a step by step optimal tree farming route?
ASSERT {"pages": ["Farming runs"]}
> wahts the normal kc you need for cg?
ASSERT {"pages": ["The Gauntlet"], "not_pages": ["Farming runs"], "not_quests": ["My Arm's Big Adventure"]}
> i mean for bowfa and ca
ASSERT {"items": ["Bow of Faerdhinen"], "pages": ["Combat Achievements", "The Gauntlet"]}

# Answer prose gets no single-word redirect guesses: a live GLM answer
# opening "Go to the Stronghold Slayer Cave" once inherited the Burthorpe
# Games Room (the wiki redirects the board game "Go") and a bare
# Stronghold page ("slayer" claims its token, orphaning "stronghold").
# Multi-word names still inherit: Meiyerditch Labs is the real
# alternative the follow-up may ask about.
what should i do for my slayer task?

ANSWER **Your task: 55 Bloodvelds remaining.**\n\n**Go to the Stronghold Slayer Cave** -- it's the best spot available to you. Meiyerditch Labs (the top location) needs partial Sins of the Father, which you haven't started.
> how do i get there?
ASSERT {"monsters": ["Bloodveld"], "quests": ["Sins of the Father"], "pages": ["Meiyerditch Laboratories"], "not_pages": ["Burthorpe Games Room", "Stronghold"]}

# (the wiki redirects "Up"). The follow-up's own two-letter slang ("ge")
# still resolves -- non-dictionary tokens keep their redirect guess.
best way to level my herblore? I need 72

ANSWER **Heads up:** Your game client shows Herblore at **level 66**, but the XP tracker reads level 78. Best option: Mastering Mixology, no herbs or secondaries needed.
> How much would that cost from the ge?
